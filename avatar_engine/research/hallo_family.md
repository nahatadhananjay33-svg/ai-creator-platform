# Hallo family (Fudan) and MEMO — academic diffusion talking heads

Verified 2026-07-04. All MIT/Apache licensed; all effectively past their
active-development peak.

## Hallo2 (ICLR 2025) — `hallo2`

3,716 stars · last push 2025-02-27 · stale. **MIT** (LICENSE verified).

- Long-duration (hour-scale) audio-driven portrait animation with
  identity-drift countermeasures; up to 4K output.
- 16 GB+ VRAM practical; heavy preprocessing chain (insightface,
  audio-separator); research-grade code.
- On the roster because long-form identity stability is exactly our
  long-narration scenario; expect install pain.

## Hallo3 (CVPR 2025) — excluded

1,391 stars · last push 2025-03-13 · stale. MIT, built on CogVideoX DiT.
Best-in-family realism and dynamic-scene handling, but 24–40 GB VRAM and
very slow generation put it outside platform serving economics.
**Excluded**; revisit only if hardware costs collapse.

## MEMO (CVPR 2025) — `memo`

1,068 stars · last push 2025-08-06 · slowing. **Apache-2.0**
(memoavatar/memo).

- Memory-guided temporal module targeting long-video identity drift;
  emotion-aware audio conditioning.
- ~16 GB VRAM, slow; smaller community. Benchmark-roster member for the
  long-narration scenario; not a lead candidate.
